Two separate cohorts — Contact creation date vs Deal event date — not a single continuous funnel. "% of Sale Completed Deals" can exceed 100% (independently-dated sets).

Year Comparison

Total Contacts / Workable Contacts / Sale Completed Rate % are Contact-Created-time cohort (recent/current year partial — use YTD mode to compare fairly). Unique Customers Sold, Sale Completed Deals, Delivered Deals, Delivery Ratio, Repeat Customers/Rate, Sales and Profit are period-activity figures (deal's own Sale Completed / Delivery date) — this avoids the current year looking artificially low just because its created-contact cohort hasn't had time to convert yet. If a Quarter/Month/From-To filter is set above, each year column shows only that slice of the year (e.g. Q1 of every year), not the full 12 months. Delivery Ratio can exceed 100% since Delivered and Sale Completed are independently-dated sets (deals booked the prior year/period can be delivered this year/period).

Contact Source Performance

Total Contacts / Workable Contacts / Conv % / Repeat % below are the Contact-Created cohort (contacts created in the selected period). Sale Completed Deals, Sales, Profit, Delivered Deals and the Avg Deal/Avg Profit columns are period-activity figures (deal's own Sale Completed date for sales, Delivery date for profit) — the same two contacts/deal populations used in the Executive Overview, shown side by side per lead source. Delivery Ratio compares two independently-dated sets (Delivered this period ÷ Sale Completed this period) so it can exceed 100% when deals booked in an earlier period get delivered this period. A row with 0 Total Contacts but nonzero deal figures means that source had no contacts created this period, only deals (from earlier-period contacts) dated this period.
